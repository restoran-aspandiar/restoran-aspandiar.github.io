# Image provenance

Original inputs are user-supplied photographs and a 10.7-second video. Only resizing and framing were used to prepare website assets. Several screenshots visibly originate from other Instagram profiles; the site labels them as photo references rather than certified Aspandiar dishes.

Generated imagery used in the website: meat-cold-platter.webp, fish-cold-platter.webp, chicken-samsa.webp, vegetable-rolls.webp, tea-sweets-cookies.webp, oriental-sweets.webp, tea-porcelain.webp, beshbarmak.webp, under `assets/`. These are illustrative serving examples, not actual restaurant photography. All are visibly labelled in Russian, Kazakh and English.

One built-in imagegen call produced a 4×3 sheet. It was cropped into individual image assets; no variants or retouching were made. The exact prompt is retained in `image-generation-prompt.txt`.

The generated savoury-pastry tile was not used for the menu's cakes. Actual cake, fruit, bread and chicken photos were cropped from the supplied video.
